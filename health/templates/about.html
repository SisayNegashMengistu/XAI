Fetal abnormalities Detection- A Explainable Detection

Exploring Explainable AI for Enhanced Detection of Fetal Anomalies in Obstetrics: Prototype Development and Validation" is crucial as it integrates Explainable AI (XAI) techniques into prototype development. XAI plays a pivotal role by not only predicting fetal anomalies but also providing transparent and interpretable insights into the AI decision-making process. This transparency is essential in healthcare settings where clinicians need to understand and trust the AI-driven diagnostic outputs. The prototype aims to demonstrate how XAI can enhance diagnostic accuracy, improve decision-making processes, and ultimately contribute to better prenatal care and maternal-fetal health outcomes .
